Enterprise AI OS is an all-in-one internal workplace app. CRM, approvals, attendance, HR, expenses, projects and knowledge live in one system, on one set of data.

What makes it different: you can just say what you want done, instead of learning which menu it lives in.

■ Say it, don't hunt for it

- "List the clients I followed up with last week"
- "Where is that travel expense claim right now"
- "Turn this lead into a client, owner Zhang Wei"

It works out what you meant, checks whether you are allowed to do it, and then actually does it. Anything that needs approval still goes through approval — the flow is never bypassed.

■ Built for the phone

- Clock in/out and field check-in, with work-hours location tracking
- Handle approvals anywhere, dictate by voice
- Photograph and upload receipts and contract attachments
- Internal messaging and push notifications

■ About location

Your work track is recorded only after you clock in, and stops the moment you clock out. While it is recording, a persistent notification stays in your notification tray — we think being tracked has to be visible. Declining location permission does not affect clocking in or any other feature.

■ About your data

The app is self-hosted: your company runs its own server and database. Your data stays with your employer — it is never pooled with ours and never shared between companies. We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising, and embed no advertising or analytics SDK.

■ Getting started

This app is for employees of companies that have deployed the system. There is no public sign-up. On first launch you enter the server address your administrator gives you (or scan their QR code), plus your phone number and password.

If your company has not deployed the system yet, get in touch.
